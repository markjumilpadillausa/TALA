Week Schedule

Weeks follow the calendar, Monday to Friday, starting from each term's first day. Change any date or deadline here, for example for holidays or suspended classes. Teachers see your schedule but cannot edit it.

Teacher Activity

Everything each teacher did in TALA, with the date and time: sign ins, PIN changes, and every LPT and PPT upload, with how early or late it was.

Teacher Accounts

Mark a practice account as a Test account to leave it out of reports, counts, and the activity summary. See each teacher's PIN, when it was changed, and the last sign in. You can set a PIN, reset it to the default, or turn an account off.

How to rename

On a computer: right click the file, choose Rename, paste the name, then press Enter. On a phone: open the file in your Files app, tap the three dots, choose Rename, then paste. Keep the ending like .docx or .pdf. Then choose the renamed file again.

Term dates and deadline

TALA uses these to show each week's dates and count how many days a file was late.

Deadline rule: LPT and PPT are due on the first class day of the week (Monday), preferably in the morning. The next day is 1 day late, the day after is 2 days late, and so on. Change a week's deadline in Week schedule.
